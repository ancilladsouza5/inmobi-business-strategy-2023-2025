# Strategic Evaluation of InMobi (2023–2025)

This project analyzes InMobi's business strategy, digital advertising ecosystem, consumer behavior trends, and AI-driven growth initiatives between 2023 and 2025. The study explores how InMobi creates value through its advertising platform, Glance, and digital content ecosystem in the evolving digital experience era.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft PowerPoint
- Secondary Research

## Key Areas Covered
- Company Overview
- Digital Advertising Market Analysis
- Consumer Behavior Insights
- Data Visualizations
- Business Model Canvas
- AIDA Model
- Strategic Recommendations

## Project Outputs
- Business Analysis Report
- Strategic Presentation
- Data Analysis Workbook
- Business Model Canvas
- AIDA Framework Analysis

## Conclusion
The project concludes that the future of digital advertising lies in personalized, AI-powered, and experience-driven platforms, with InMobi demonstrating strong potential for sustained growth and innovation.
